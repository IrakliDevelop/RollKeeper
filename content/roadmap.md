## Built

- A D&D 5e character sheet in the 2014 or 2024 layout that saves in your browser as you play
- Hit points, spell slots, concentration, conditions, rests, class resources and heroic inspiration
- Guided level-ups, multiclassing, inventory, notes and a character calendar
- Virtual 3D dice on the sheet; the results stay on your screen
- Export and import characters as a file, or share one with a link or QR code for 24 hours
- Campaigns with a join code, an encounter and initiative tracker, an NPC library and custom monsters
- A battle map for a shared screen: tokens, area templates, measuring and fog of war
- During a session, the DM sees players' hit points, conditions and concentration, and players see the campaign's calendar and locations
- A rules reference: spells, monsters, classes and feats

## In progress

- Restyling the DM screens, the rules reference and the battle map tools to match the character sheet

## Next

- Saving characters and campaigns on a server, so they open on any device
- Scenes and a table display for the shared screen

## Later

- A full virtual tabletop for groups that don't share a room
- Dice rolls that the DM sees

## Not built yet

- Accounts, cloud backup or saving across devices. Until server saving (under Next) is built, characters stay in this browser
